## Welcome to the Netherlands Opportunity Lab

The Netherlands Opportunity Lab is a research-driven initiative focused on understanding inequality, opportunity structures, and data-driven policy insights in the Netherlands.

We develop and communicate scientific research using administrative and linked microdata, with a focus on education, health, and socioeconomic outcomes.
