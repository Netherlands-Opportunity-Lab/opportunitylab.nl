The monitor accompanying the first progress letter of the National Programme on Poverty and Debt (*Nationaal Programma Armoede en Schulden*) uses KansenKaart to show intergenerational poverty. Figure 26, "KansenKaart intergenerationele armoede", compares the average personal income of people whose parents had a low income with that of people whose parents had a high income, for a selection of large, medium-sized and small municipalities, based on where they grew up. The figure cites [KansenKaart.nl](https://kansenkaart.nl) as its source.

The monitor is annex 2 to the progress letter the Minister of Social Affairs and Employment sent to Parliament on 10 March 2026.
